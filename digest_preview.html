🔥 MUST READ ScienceDaily Top 18m ago
This simple amino acid supplement greatly reduces Alzheimer’s damage

Researchers from Kindai University found that arginine — an inexpensive, widely available amino acid — suppressed toxic amyloid-β aggregation both in lab tests and in two animal models of Alzheimer's (Drosophila and knock-in mice). Treated mice showed reduced amyloid plaques, better behavioral test performance, and lower expression of pro-inflammatory cytokine genes. The findings position arginine as a low-cost candidate for rapid clinical translation, given its established safety profile.

💡 Takeaway: A safe, over-the-counter amino acid reduced Alzheimer's hallmark brain pathology in animal models — a promising shortcut to clinical trials via drug repositioning.

🔥 MUST READ ScienceDaily Top 1h ago
Scientists turn plastic waste into clean hydrogen fuel using sunlight

Adelaide University researchers demonstrated that solar-powered photoreforming can convert plastic waste into hydrogen and valuable industrial chemicals using light-sensitive photocatalysts at low temperatures. Early experiments produced high hydrogen yields and ran continuously for over 100 hours. However, scaling faces major hurdles: mixed plastic types and additives interfere with the process, photocatalysts degrade over time, and separating the resulting gas-liquid mix is energy-intensive.

💡 Takeaway: Turning plastic waste into clean hydrogen with sunlight addresses pollution and energy simultaneously, but catalyst durability and real-world plastic complexity remain the key barriers to scale.
